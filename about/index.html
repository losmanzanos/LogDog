Who we are

We have been friends since the days of Trapper Keepers and Tamagotchis. After years of building things and getting better at carpentry, we decided to do it together and put our time into timber framing.

Log Dog Timberworks is the result. We work with you on the design, cut every joint ourselves, and raise the frame on your property.

Some people want a cozy retreat. Others want the yard everyone ends up in on a Saturday. Either way the frame is built the same: solid timber, cut and pinned by hand.
